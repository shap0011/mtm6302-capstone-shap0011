How to Play

Clicking on Pokémon will display a larger image of the Pokémon and additional details about the Pokémon. A "Load More" button allows to load the next 20 Pokémon on the page. You can mark Pokémon as caught by clicking on the white circle with a pink dot. You can release the caught Pokémon by clicking on the pink circle with a white dot.
